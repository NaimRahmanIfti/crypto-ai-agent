The desk takes every GO idea as a demo trade in a $10,000 paper account, with the same risk rules as real money, and manages it to its stop, target or time limit using real prices, fees and slippage. Ideas the gate blocked are traded too, in a separate shadow book with no money, so you can see whether the gate is right. Nothing is ever sent to a broker.
